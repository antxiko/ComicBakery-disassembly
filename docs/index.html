A 92-column bakery whose machines change from one stage to the next, an eight-minute working day, and the raccoons the listing called carts, drawn from the ROM.

Konami - (c) Konami 1984 - An RC-714 16 KB cartridge - MSX1 - page 1 - Dump 089e6cd7...

Not one capture here. Every picture is drawn from the bytes of the ROM: the screens and the bakery with the cartridge's own set-up chain rewritten in Python, and the baker and the raccoons with their pose and step tables, checked against openMSX down to zero bytes. The listing and the numbers are reproducible with make, and reassembling gives back the ROM byte for byte.
